Institution Profile

Our School

St. Thomas Higher Secondary School, Kiliyanthara is an aided educational institution run by the Catholic minority community with all the rights and privileges granted by the Constitution of India, and recognized by the Department of General Education of Kerala State.

Located amidst the scenic and peaceful surroundings of Kiliyanthara in the Iritty educational sub-district of Kannur, the institution has been serving students from diverse backgrounds for decades, preparing them for higher academic pursuits and responsible citizenship.

Official Registration:
• High School Section Code: 14059 (Established 1964)
• Higher Secondary Section Code: 13155 (Established 2010)
• UDISE Code: 32020901809

Aim and Objective

Our school is primarily meant to provide children with a sound education based on moral values. It is open to all, irrespective of religion, caste, or community, paying deep respect to their religious freedom and freedom of conscience.

We strive to foster intellectual curiosity, physical health, emotional balance, and moral conviction, ensuring every student blossoms into an enlightened individual ready to serve society.

Our Inspiration

St. Thomas was one of the twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ. He who was a dedicated follower of Jesus remains as our perpetual source of inspiration, as the influence of his faith, courage, and vision permeates through all our activities today.

Key Campus Facilities

Creating an enriching environment for academic and personal growth.

Smart Classrooms

Equipped with modern multimedia systems to enable interactive, engaging, and comprehensive learning for students across classes.

Equipped Science Labs

Three dedicated and fully equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology to support practical scientific inquiry.

School Library

An enriched collection of curriculum texts, reference volumes, periodicals, and educational magazines fostering healthy reading habits.

Computer Laboratory

High-speed computers and internet access supporting IT education, practical coding, and digital literacy.

School Auditorium

A spacious assembly auditorium with seating capacity for over 350 people, hosting academic seminars, assemblies, and cultural events.

Student Counselling

Dedicated counselling programmes to support student well-being, personal development, and career guidance.
