St. Thomas Higher Secondary School, Kiliyanthara is an aided educational institution run by the Catholic minority community under the Corporate Educational Agency of the Archdiocese of Tellicherry, recognized by the General Education Department of the Government of Kerala. We provide students with a sound education grounded in academic excellence, moral integrity, and social responsibility.

National Service Scheme (NSS) Unit

Our school maintains an active National Service Scheme (NSS) unit affiliated with the Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE) NSS Cell, Kerala. The unit instills civic responsibility, environmental awareness, campus greening, organic farming, and community upliftment through regular projects and annual residential camps.

Academic & Co-Curricular Highlights

St. Thomas Higher Secondary School has consistently maintained commendable standards in SSLC and Higher Secondary public examinations conducted by the Government of Kerala.

  • High Examination Pass Percentage: Outstanding performance in Plus Two Science and Commerce streams.
  • Active Clubs: National Service Scheme (NSS), Nature Club, Science Club, and IT Club.
  • District & Sub-District Competitions: Regular participation in Kerala School Kalolsavam, Science Mela, and Sports Meets.
  • Values-Based Education: Fostering civic discipline, social concern, and spiritual integrity.
