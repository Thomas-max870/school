Curriculum & Academic Structure

Providing high-quality education aligned with Kerala State Board standards.

General Education Department

1. High School Section (Classes VIII to X)

School Code: 14059

The High School section at St. Thomas HSS, Kiliyanthara follows the curriculum prescribed by the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Kerala, preparing students thoroughly for the Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC) public examinations.

Component Subjects Taught Assessment Mode
First Language Malayalam (Part 1 & 2) Continuous Evaluation (CE) & Board Exam
Second & Third Languages English, Hindi Continuous Evaluation (CE) & Board Exam
Core Disciplines Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Social Science CE, Practical Lab Work & Board Exam
Co-scholastic Areas Physical Education, Art & Work Experience, IT (Information Technology) Internal Continuous Evaluation
Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE)

2. Higher Secondary Section (Plus One & Plus Two)

HSS Code: 13155

The Higher Secondary section was sanctioned and established in 2010 to extend quality secondary education to rural and regional students. We offer verified streams in Science and Commerce, taught by qualified postgraduate teachers with well-equipped laboratory facilities.

A. Science Stream (Combinations Offered)

Part Subject Area Subjects
Part I Compulsory Language English
Part II Second Language (Any one) Malayalam / Hindi
Part III Core Science Disciplines Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology (Botany & Zoology)
Also supported by Computer Science elective in designated batches.

B. Commerce Stream (Combinations Offered)

Part Subject Area Subjects
Part I Compulsory Language English
Part II Second Language (Any one) Malayalam / Hindi
Part III Core Commerce Disciplines Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Computer Applications / Mathematics

Academic Clubs & Practical Activities

Fostering hands-on learning, scientific exploration, and intellectual curiosity.

Science Club

Organizes annual science fairs, working model exhibitions, quiz competitions, and experimental workshops to nurture scientific inquiry.

IT Club

Equips students with computer literacy, coding basics, and multimedia editing skills utilizing our networked computer lab.

Nature Club

Focuses on ecological preservation, biodiversity awareness, campus flora documentation, and tree planting initiatives.

NSS (Service Scheme)

Provides civic training, disaster awareness, leadership skills, and community upliftment through active field projects.

[Notice for Academic Inquiries] Admissions to Plus One classes are conducted through the centralized Single Window System (HSCAP) governed by the Directorate of Higher Secondary Education, Government of Kerala. For specific institutional quota inquiries, please contact the school office.
