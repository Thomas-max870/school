Official Bulletin • St. Thomas HSS Kiliyanthara
  • 01
    Jun
    Commencement of the New Academic Session & Praveshanotsavam
    The school reopens for the new academic year for all High School classes (Standard VIII to X) and Second Year Higher Secondary classes (Plus Two). Praveshanotsavam celebrations will be held in the school auditorium from 9:30 AM.
    Academic Issued by: Office of the Principal
  • 15
    Jul
    Plus One Single Window (HSCAP) Admission Schedule
    Candidates allotted to St. Thomas HSS, Kiliyanthara (School Code 13155) for First Year Higher Secondary Science and Commerce streams are instructed to report to the office with original certificates, TC, conduct certificate, and allotment memo as per the DHSE timetable.
    Admissions HSS Code: 13155
  • 24
    Sep
    NSS Day Observance & Social Work Programme
    The National Service Scheme unit of St. Thomas HSS will observe National NSS Day. The morning session includes a volunteer oath ceremony followed by a campus cleanliness and environmental maintenance initiative under the motto "Not Me But You".
    NSS Unit Program Officer: NSS Cell
  • 10
    Dec
    Second Terminal / Half-Yearly Examinations
    Second terminal examinations for High School and Higher Secondary sections will commence as per the timetable published by the General Education Department. Timetable copies are displayed on the main corridor notice board.
    Examinations Applies to: Standards VIII to XII
  • 05
    Jan
    Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) General Body Meeting
    The general body meeting of the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) will convene in the school auditorium at 2:00 PM to review student academic progress, discuss infrastructure upgrades, and plan upcoming school events.
    General Convenor: PTA Executive
