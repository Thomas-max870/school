St. Thomas HSS National Service Scheme Unit

The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit at St. Thomas Higher Secondary School, Kiliyanthara operates under the Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE), Government of Kerala. NSS aims to develop students' personality and civic sense through voluntary community service, fostering empathy, national integration, and hands-on social responsibility.

About the NSS Unit

The National Service Scheme provides a constructive platform for Plus One and Plus Two students to engage directly with real-world community challenges. The motto "Not Me But You" reflects the essence of democratic living and upholds the need for selfless service.

Volunteers learn to appreciate each other's point of view and show consideration for other fellow human beings. At St. Thomas HSS, the unit coordinates continuous community programmes throughout the academic year and conducts an annual 7-day residential special camp.

Key Educational Goals of NSS:
  • Understand the community in which students work and their relationship to it.
  • Identify the problems of the community and involve students in problem-solving.
  • Develop among students a sense of social and civic responsibility.
  • Acquire leadership qualities and democratic attitudes.

Core NSS Activities & Programmes

Meaningful service projects carried out on campus and in surrounding rural communities.

Environment

Eco-Restoration & Green Campus

Massive sapling plantation drives on World Environment Day, maintenance of school botanical and medicinal garden, and elimination of single-use plastics to ensure an eco-friendly learning environment.

Agriculture

Organic Farming Initiative

NSS volunteers prepare organic vegetable plots on the school campus, cultivating indigenous vegetables without harmful pesticides to promote healthy agriculture and food security among students.

Community

Village Outreach & Cleanliness

Regular sanitation drives under the Swachh Bharat mission across local public paths, water bodies, and public health centers around Kiliyanthara and Vilamana village.

Health

Blood Donation & Health Camps

Voluntary blood donor registry drives, lifestyle disease awareness seminars, first aid demonstrations, and hygiene workshops conducted in collaboration with local health authorities.

Civic Life

National Observances

Active celebration of National Service Scheme Day (September 24), Independence Day, Republic Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and National Integration Day through debates, rallies, and pledge-taking ceremonies.

Disaster Awareness

Community Safety & Relief

Training sessions in disaster management, road safety orientation in coordination with the Motor Vehicles Department, and volunteer support during regional flood and weather alerts.

Flagship Programme

Seven-Day Annual Special Residential Camp

Annual Winter / Vacation Camp

The 7-Day Special Residential Camp is the pinnacle of the NSS calendar. Volunteers stay together in an adopted village or designated institution, experiencing communal living, manual labor, cultural exchange, and direct rural engagement.

Shramadanam (Physical Labor)

Renovation of public pathways, drainage clearing, and rural water conservation work.

Socio-Economic Surveys

Door-to-door survey of the adopted locality to assess health, water, and educational needs.

Personality & Leadership

Daily evening cultural programmes, yoga, meditation, debates, and lectures by invited resource persons.
