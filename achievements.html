School Achievements

Excellence Across Academics, Sports & Arts

Recognizing the milestones, dedication, and honors earned by our students and school community.

Academics

Public Examination Excellence

St. Thomas Higher Secondary School takes pride in its academic rigor. The school consistently achieves high pass percentages in both the SSLC (Class X) examinations and the Higher Secondary Public Examinations (DHSE Plus Two).

  • Consistent high pass rates in Plus Two Science and Commerce streams.
  • Numerous full A+ grades achieved by students across rural Kannur district.
  • Systematic remedial support and special mentoring for slow learners.
  • Active participation in state-level talent searches and mathematics olympiads.
Social Service

NSS Unit Commendations

Our National Service Scheme volunteers have earned commendations for their tireless community work in Kiliyanthara and neighboring rural panchayats:

  • Conduct of successful annual 7-day residential community service camps.
  • Comprehensive organic vegetable farming initiatives on school campus.
  • Plastic eradication and clean environment campaigns in collaboration with the local panchayat.
  • Active volunteer blood donor directory maintenance.
Culture & Arts

School Kalolsavam & Cultural Meets

Students regularly participate in sub-district and revenue district Kerala School Kalolsavam competitions, showcasing Kerala's rich artistic traditions.

  • Participation in classical and folk dance competitions.
  • Elocution, debate, and recitation in Malayalam, English, and Hindi.
  • Literary writing competitions including short stories, poetry, and essay contests.
  • School annual day cultural exhibitions and stage presentations.
Physical Education

Sports & Athletics

Physical education is an integral part of the school curriculum, encouraging teamwork, discipline, and healthy competition:

  • Participation in sub-district school athletics and track events.
  • Annual school sports meet fostering house-wise athletic competitions.
  • Inter-class and inter-school football and volleyball tournaments.
  • Yoga, physical fitness drills, and health awareness sessions.

Key Institutional Milestones

Year Milestone / Event Impact
1964 Establishment of St. Thomas High School Brought structured high school education to the children of Kiliyanthara and nearby migrant farming communities.
2010 Sanctioning of Higher Secondary Section (DHSE) Enabled students to complete Plus One and Plus Two in Science and Commerce locally without travelling distant towns.
Ongoing Smart Classrooms & Laboratory Modernization Equipped physics, chemistry, biology, and computer labs for modern scientific investigation.
Ongoing Active DHSE NSS Unit Program Provides hundreds of higher secondary volunteers with leadership training and rural community outreach.
